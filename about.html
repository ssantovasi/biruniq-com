About

Who builds BirunIQ, what we hold ourselves to, and where to find our legal terms.

Who we are

BirunIQ is a sovereign geospatial intelligence platform, offered by Bitruvius, Inc. It turns satellite, aerial, sensor, registry and field observation into decisions an agency or operator can defend, across many industries from one engine.

What we hold ourselves to

  • Air-gap capable. The platform can run entirely inside a customer’s own environment with no connection outside it. The software contains no telemetry, crash reporting or online licence check.
  • Your data stays yours. Customers keep all rights in their data, and we do not use it to train models without their written agreement.
  • Provenance that holds. Every model the platform runs is recorded with its source, version, weight hashes and licence, and model-generated data is marked as generated.
  • Licence hygiene. Third-party data, models and software are used under their licences and credited as those licences require.

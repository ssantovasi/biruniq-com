Sign in as yourself

Each person connects their own ArcGIS Online or ArcGIS Enterprise account as a Named User. Esri's permissions decide what they see, and no Esri token ever reaches the browser.

Available today

Living Atlas, files and services

Living Atlas layers through GeoWizard; file geodatabases, shapefiles, KML and KMZ; Esri REST feature, map and image services, WMS, WMTS and WFS. Credits are kept.

Available today

Style, filter and popups

Icon markers, clustering, colour by attribute, line and polygon styles, attribute filters and full feature popups on ArcGIS layers. The ArcGIS layer itself is unchanged.

Available today

Point clouds, splats and 3D buildings

LAS and LAZ point clouds, 3D Tiles and Gaussian splats beside your ArcGIS layers, and 3D buildings with each building's own popup.

Coming soon

3D globe and scene layers

Your account layers on the 3D globe, and your organisation's scene layers, including point cloud scene layers.

Coming soon

Bitruvius ScorpioOne Spatial Engine

One engine under everything BirunIQ moves, inside your deployment: Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF, the Turbo codecs, and I3S and 3D Tiles writers that are ahead of schedule and nearing readiness.

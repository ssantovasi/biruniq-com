Industry served

African Union states and continental agencies

Fifty-four states, the fastest urban growth on earth, and a base-map dependency that has historically been licensed from outside the continent.

One Africa carries continental programmes in one place: command, food and agriculture, health and immunisation, borders, ports, land use and land cover, flood and climate risk, and settlements.

In the trailer it runs a port: vessels, terminals and berth occupancy.

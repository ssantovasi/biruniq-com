City and national government

A government is judged on four things at once — whether the city works, whether the coast is secure, whether the lights come on in a crisis, and whether anyone can prove it afterwards.

Atlas is the general-government edition. It puts a city's sensors, assets, incidents and responding units on one map: each sensor with its 24-hour trend, each asset with its record.

Maritime traffic sits in the same picture, with every vessel's stored track, and a mapbook can be generated straight from the current view.

Department modules

5
each owned and accountable on its own.

01Smart City

Streetlights, waste, permits and service requests on one municipal map, each with its service level attached.

02Situational Awareness 3D

The city as a navigable 3D scene — sightlines, shadow, elevation and floor-level detail for planning and response.

03Emergency Services

Station coverage, response isochrones and live incident load across fire, police and ambulance together.

04Maritime

Ports, channels, berths and coastal assets held as a managed operational layer rather than a chart.

05Maritime Domain Awareness

Vessel traffic, anchorage behaviour and approach monitoring across the territorial sea.

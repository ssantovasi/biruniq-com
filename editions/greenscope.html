Industry served

Environmental regulators and ESG-accountable operators

Environmental regulation is an evidence problem.

GreenScope keeps environmental findings with their inspection photos and risk by zone, alongside permitting, biodiversity and habitat change, and soil intelligence.

Its groundwater view carries over thirteen hundred public monitoring wells, clustered by count. Zoom in and they open into well records, even over satellite imagery.

Department modules

8
each owned and accountable on its own.

01Environmental Command

The portfolio compliance picture — and the records the numbers cannot see, stated on the board.

02Inspection Reports

Findings by severity and topic, with corrective actions, overdue tracking and recurrence.

03Permitting

Conditions, expiries and obligations bound to the asset that triggers them.

04Biodiversity & Habitat

Protected habitat and species constraints on the same map as the work.

05Habitat Change

What the habitat did between epochs — adjudicated, not asserted.

06Groundwater

Aquifer condition, abstraction and contamination pathways below the site.

07Soil Intelligence

Soil condition and contamination, sampled and interpolated with its uncertainty carried.

08Predictive Risk

Where the next finding is most likely, from the pattern of the ones already closed.

Every board states its own denominator — what was counted, what was excluded, and why

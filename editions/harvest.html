Industry served

Ministries of agriculture and food security

A ministry has to state national production before the harvest is in, and defend the number afterwards.

Harvest brings a crop calendar, a crop index from Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, and field boundaries delineated by the model. The boundaries in the trailer are real model output, not demonstration data.

Crop type and land use, crop health, yield forecast, water and drought, the field registry, and risk and finance complete the edition.

Department modules

7
each owned and accountable on its own.

01Harvest Command

The national crop picture for a ministry — area, condition and outlook in one view.

02Crop Type & Land Use

What is planted where, resolved from Sentinel-2 rather than taken on declaration.

03Crop Health

Vigour and stress by field, through the season rather than at the end of it.

04Yield Forecast

Expected production against area and condition, updated as the season runs.

05Water & Drought

Moisture deficit, irrigation demand and drought exposure by district.

06Field Boundaries & Registry

The field fabric itself — boundaries, holders and registry linkage.

07Risk & Finance

Insurance, credit and subsidy exposure attached to the field it was written against.

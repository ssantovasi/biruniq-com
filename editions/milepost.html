Industry served

State and national transport departments

A DOT is accountable at two altitudes at once: a commissioner answering for a multi-year capital programme, and a crew answering for today’s lane closure.

Milepost is the transportation-department edition: bridge and facility inspections, pavement management, lane striping, live traffic, roadwork and obstructions, major projects, crew status, snow removal and emergency response.

Department modules

10
each owned and accountable on its own.

01Executive Overview

The whole programme at commissioner altitude — spend, schedule and condition by district.

02Pavement Management

Condition index by segment, deterioration curves, and the treatment that is actually due.

03Facility Management

Depots, salt sheds, rest areas and buildings managed as a single estate.

04Lane Striping

Marking retroreflectivity, wear and the repaint programme by route.

05Live Traffic

Current speed, volume and incident state across the whole network.

06Roadwork & Obstructions

Every active closure, lane restriction and permit on the road at the same time.

07Major Projects

Capital delivery tracked spatially, with schedule and stakeholder exposure attached.

08Crew Status

Where the crews are, what they are assigned, and what they have completed today.

09Snow Removal & Treatment

Route coverage, material usage and pass completion during a live event.

10Emergency Response

Incident command over the roadway, with detour and closure control.

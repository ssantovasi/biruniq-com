Industry served

Airport authorities and operators

An airport is two businesses on one piece of land: a movement area governed by safety regulation, and a property portfolio governed by revenue.

Tower is the airport edition. It keeps airfield lighting fixture by fixture with each one's condition, along with surface condition, lane striping, signage, property leases, major projects, emergency services, vehicle and air traffic, and weather.

Department modules

10
each owned and accountable on its own.

01Air Traffic

Movement areas, approach corridors and traffic load rendered over the live airfield.

02Surface Condition

Pavement condition, contamination, friction and closures across runway, taxiway and apron.

03Tarmac Lighting

Every fixture, circuit and outage on the airfield lighting system, with maintenance state.

04Lane Striping

Markings inventory, wear and repaint scheduling across movement and non-movement areas.

05Signage

Guidance and mandatory instruction signs with their compliance and legibility status.

06Property Lease

Every leasehold, concession and parcel on airport land, with term, revenue and obligation.

07Major Projects

Capital works on the airfield, phased against operational impact and closure windows.

08Emergency Services

ARFF coverage, response routes and hot-spot readiness across the aerodrome.

09Vehicle Traffic

Landside and airside vehicle movement, gate load and incursion risk.

10Weather

Live conditions over the airfield, joined to surface condition and operational restriction.

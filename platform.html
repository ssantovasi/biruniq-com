BirunIQ Field

The last mile is a person, offline.

No-code field apps with offline capture and sync. The inspector's photograph becomes a feature in the department's operating picture without an import step, and without a second system to license.

Without Field

Five hand-offs. Three days.

  1. Paper form
  2. Phone photo
  3. Email to the office
  4. Re-key into GIS
  5. Someone hopes it was the right pole
With BirunIQ Field

One hand-off. Minutes.

  1. Capture in the field, offline
  2. Syncs on return to coverage
  3. Appears as a feature in the module: located, permissioned, audited
Configured, not coded

A field app is a configuration of an existing module, so it inherits that module's permissions and schema.

Offline by design

Capture without connectivity, sync when the vehicle returns to coverage.

No second system

No separate field product to license, and no second copy of the asset record to reconcile.

Evidence, not anecdote

Every capture is located, timestamped and attributed.

Incident record
Record · two clocks

What we knew, and when we knew it.

Every incident record carries two clocks: when something was true in the world, and when the system learned it. Corrections supersede; they never overwrite, and that is enforced at the database.

Provenance

Know where every answer came from.

Every model the platform runs has a manifest entry: source, commit, weight hashes, licence text and fetch date. Anything a model synthesised rather than observed is marked, and the mark follows it to whatever is derived from it.

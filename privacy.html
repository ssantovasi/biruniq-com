Privacy Policy

How Bitruvius, Inc. collects, uses and protects personal information across our websites and the Cartos platform.

Effective: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bitruvius, Inc. (“Bitruvius”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit our websites (including biruniq.com), contact us, receive text messages from us, or use the Cartos geospatial intelligence platform, which we also offer under the BirunIQ name (the “Platform”).

1. Scope and our role

  • Websites and enquiries. For our websites, enquiries and marketing communications, we decide how personal information is used and are responsible for it.
  • The hosted Platform. When an organisation (our “Customer”) uses the Platform as a service we host, we process the personal information in its account and content on the Customer’s behalf and under its instructions. The Customer is responsible for that information, and questions about it should go to the Customer first.
  • On-premises and air-gapped deployments. When a Customer runs the Platform in its own environment, the software sends no telemetry, usage data, crash reports or licence checks to us, and we receive none of the data in it. We access such a deployment only if the Customer grants us access for support, and only for that purpose.

2. Information we collect

When you visit our websites or contact us

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, organisation, phone number and the content of your messages when you contact us or request a briefing or demo.
  • Technical information: standard web server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, kept by our hosting and content-delivery providers.
  • Browser storage: where a page of our website is access-restricted, we store a flag in your browser’s session storage so you are not asked for the password on every page. Our websites set no advertising or analytics cookies and use no third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Web fonts: some of our web pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when it serves them.

When you use the Platform

  • Account information: your name, email address, username, organisation, team memberships and roles.
  • Sign-in and security information: password hashes, multi-factor authentication enrolments (such as an authenticator app, a passkey or device sign-in, email codes or, where enabled, SMS codes), and the session cookies and tokens needed to keep you signed in. These cookies are strictly necessary for the Platform to work.
  • Customer content: the data you and your organisation upload, create or connect, such as geospatial layers, imagery, field observations (which can include photographs and locations), inspection and incident records, reports, and the questions you ask the Platform’s assistant features.
  • Logs: security and audit records of actions taken in the Platform, including IP address, time and the action performed.

When you receive text messages

  • Your mobile number, and the date, time and method by which you opted in to or out of messages.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, secure and support the Platform and our websites.
  • To authenticate you and protect accounts, including sending one-time sign-in codes and password-reset messages.
  • To send service and administrative notices, and the text messages you have agreed to receive.
  • To respond to your enquiries and to arrange briefings, demos and evaluations.
  • To detect, investigate and prevent abuse, fraud and security incidents.
  • To comply with law and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use Customer content to advertise to anyone, and we do not use it to train or improve machine-learning models without the Customer’s written agreement.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only as follows:

  • Service providers who process it for us under contract and only for the services they provide to us: cloud hosting (DigitalOcean), website hosting and content delivery (Cloudflare and GitHub), email delivery (Resend) and SMS delivery (Twilio, and the mobile carriers it uses).
  • AI model provider. Where a hosted Platform deployment has the assistant features enabled, the text of your question and the map context needed to answer it are sent to our AI model provider (Anthropic) to generate the response. Deployments with these features disabled, and air-gapped deployments, send nothing.
  • Map and data providers. When you view a layer served directly by a third-party provider (for example satellite imagery, street-level imagery, 3D tiles, traffic or vessel data), your browser or our servers request it from that provider, which receives your IP address and the area requested. Each provider’s own privacy policy applies to those requests. A Customer’s administrator can turn external data off for their organisation.
  • Your organisation. Administrators of your organisation’s Platform account can see account information and activity within that account.
  • Legal and safety reasons: where required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

5. Text messaging and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. We share mobile numbers only with the service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf, and only for that purpose. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

6. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy. Account information is kept while the account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards. Customer content is kept for the term of the Customer’s agreement and then deleted or returned as that agreement provides. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis. We keep records for longer where the law requires it.

7. Security

We protect personal information with administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity, including encryption in transit, isolation of each Customer’s data enforced in the database itself, multi-factor authentication, role-based access controls and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected Customers and individuals of a security breach as the law requires.

8. International transfers

We and our service providers may process personal information in the United States and in other countries where those providers operate. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as standard contractual clauses. A Customer that needs its data to remain in a particular country can run the Platform in its own environment.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You will not be treated differently for exercising these rights. To make a request, contact us at the address below; if your information is held in a Customer’s Platform account, we will refer your request to that Customer and help it respond. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

Our websites and the Platform are intended for businesses and public agencies. They are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and where the changes are material we will give notice through the Platform or by email.

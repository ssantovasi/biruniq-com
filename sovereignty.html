Sovereignty

Your world. Inside your boundary.

“Your world” in the tagline is literal: your ground, your data, inside your boundary. BirunIQ deploys three ways, and the rules that matter hold in all three.

Connected

SaaS

Hosted for you, on the same engine as every other deployment. External data flows only through the egress gate, and each tenant can switch external data off for itself.

Connected

Sovereign hybrid

For customers whose data must stay under their own control, with external data still behind the gate and the same per-tenant switch.

Lockout

Air-gap capable

A per-customer build for customers who need a lockout. No runtime call leaves the deployment boundary: no external data, no model API, no CDN font. A delivery is called air-gapped only after it passes verification on that build.

The egress gate

External data, on your terms.

In a connected deployment, external data passes through one gate. Every external feed is listed with its status, and a tenant can switch external data off from the admin console.

Separation

Kept apart where it counts.

Database

Row-level security

Access

Scoped by team

Inside a tenant, access follows the team, not the whole organisation.

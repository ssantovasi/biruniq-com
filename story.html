The name

The platform that measures the Earth, named for the man who measured it first.

Abu Rayhan al-Biruni was an 11th-century scholar. From a summit of known height in present-day Pakistan, he sighted the horizon and measured the dip angle θ between the horizontal and his line of sight.

That line is tangent to the Earth: it touches it at exactly one point. From one triangle he recovered the radius of the entire planet, a thousand years before satellites.

R = h · cos θ / (1 − cos θ)
